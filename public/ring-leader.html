Clask: the launch four departments cheered
The Dev Team, led by Tedane Blake, built the real-time chat that lets agents get help without putting customers on hold. The Call Center Operations Team, through Diana Ricottone, led the rollout alongside Annisha and the Quality Control Team — getting everyone trained on the platform and keeping every chat brief, focused, and customer-first.
Before Clask, an agent who needed help had to put the caller on hold or reach for a personal phone to page a supervisor. Now the answer arrives in-chat: holds are down, the call center moves faster, and because customers are billed by the minute, shorter handling time lands straight on the invoice. A win in every direction.
